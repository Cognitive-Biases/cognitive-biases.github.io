# Improve the existing English library

Keep the 216 canonical English explanation URLs stable. A source-backed explanation, a valid worked example and a useful distinction are improvements; extra words and extra URLs are not outcomes.

## Current publication changes

- Render maintained explanation headings and bullet lists as semantic sections and lists, for both older and newer editorial descriptions. Preserve unsupported or unrecognised prose without inventing headings or claims.
- Align six older openings with their existing evidence reviews: conservatism, hindsight, impact, outcome, projection and subadditivity. Label illustrative examples and give inspectable checks rather than promises to remove a bias.
- Rewrite the subadditivity guide around a valid probability partition. Separate unpacking from new evidence, overlapping risks from mutually exclusive categories, and consistency from forecast accuracy.
- Put long-form provenance before the narrative. A concept's evidence review does not independently verify every statement in a restored article. Only an explicitly scoped, dated content review qualifies for the revised-article label.
- Give reviewed articles a direct link to evidence and limitations before the long-form text.
