# Translation state

Cognitive Biases treats translation as a managed content state, not as a second source of truth.

- Canonical scientific content lives in English.
- German, Russian, French, Brazilian Portuguese, and Spanish wording may be reviewed and naturalized without creating new concepts.
- IDs, evidence status, source lineage and relations remain language-agnostic.
- The reviewed human interface is published in English, French and Spanish, with an initial reviewed Brazilian Portuguese subset; German and Russian entries also contain reviewed localized layers while some public surfaces remain architecture-specific.
- Where a locale does not publish reviewed content for an item, canonical English remains the reference rather than being presented as reviewed localized copy.

Translation review and evidence review are separate states. A natural localized explanation must preserve the strength, uncertainty and boundaries of the canonical scientific record; localization never upgrades an evidence class.
