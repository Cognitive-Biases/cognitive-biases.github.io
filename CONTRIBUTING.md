# Contributing to Cognitive Biases

Useful corrections are welcome. Publication is an editorial decision, not an automatic result of opening an issue or pull request.

## Choose the right path

Use the GitHub issue forms for:

- a factual correction;
- a new or better source;
- a taxonomy or concept proposal;
- a data/schema bug;
- a research collaboration.

For commercial reuse or a product partnership, use the contact path on the public Partnerships page instead of mixing a licensing request with an evidence correction.

## Evidence proposals

Please include enough information for another person to check the claim:

1. the canonical page or concept ID;
2. the exact claim that may need to change;
3. the source, preferably with DOI, PMID or another stable identifier;
4. what the source actually supports and what it does not establish;
5. any important population, task or measurement limits.

A new paper does not automatically overturn a review. We compare it with the existing evidence before changing the public conclusion.

## Original writing and copyright

Write explanations in your own words. Do not paste large passages from papers, books or websites. Short quotations should be used only when necessary and when they are copyright-safe.

By contributing original material, you agree that accepted contributions may be published under the repository's current content licence. Third-party material keeps its own rights and must not be relicensed by accident.

## AI-assisted drafts

AI tools can help prepare a proposal, find possible gaps or improve wording. A machine-generated draft cannot mark itself as human reviewed or evidence reviewed. Sources and claims still need editorial checking.

## Review states

Evidence-like contributions normally move through:

`proposed -> research review -> editorial review -> published / rejected`

`Rejected` does not mean the contribution was useless. It can mean the evidence is too weak, the concept overlaps with an existing one, the claim is out of scope, or publication would create more confusion than value.

## Schema changes

Public data consumers depend on the versioned contract in `docs/data-contract.md`. A breaking schema change must explain the migration path and update the major `schemaVersion`.

## Safety and scope

This is educational information about judgment and decision making. Do not submit diagnostic, medical, legal, financial or mental-health treatment claims as ordinary bias guidance.
